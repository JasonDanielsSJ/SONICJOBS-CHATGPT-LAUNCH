The Problem SonicJobs Solves
Most inbound hiring funnels
are broken by design.

High abandonment. Bot submissions. Incomplete screener data. No way to verify who is actually applying. These are not random occurrences — they are predictable outcomes of a redirect-based apply architecture that has not changed in 20 years.

The Problem

Redirect-based apply flows create compounding quality and efficiency problems.

✕
95% of candidates abandon before completingThe redirect is the exit point. Most candidates who are asked to leave one platform and start over in another never return. The drop-off is consistent across industries and role types.
✕
Multiple platforms, multiple friction pointsA typical apply flow involves credential prompts, page redirects, and repeated form contexts. Each step is a compounding source of abandonment for candidates who have other options.
✕
Rising bot volume, declining pipeline qualityAI-generated applications have increased inbound volume without a corresponding increase in qualified candidates advancing past the first screen. Your ATS is full. Your pipeline is not.
✕
No way to verify who is actually applyingExisting apply flows have no structural mechanism for confirming that a submission came from a real person who genuinely wants the role. You are screening on faith.
✕
More screening work, fewer qualified hiresRecruiters are spending more time per qualified hire than at any point in recent years — a direct result of signal degradation in the inbound funnel.
